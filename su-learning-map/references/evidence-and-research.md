# Evidence and research protocol

## Evidence classes

| Class | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| `primary` | authored work, full first-person interview, official record | direct support with locator |
| `corroborated` | stable pattern repeated across primary sources | synthesis with multiple sources |
| `external` | credible criticism or third-party observation | boundary, contrast, or dispute |
| `inference` | new conclusion generated from distilled models | label as inference, never as quotation |
| `current` | time-sensitive market, product, policy, or recent behavior | browse and date-stamp |

## Person research routes

Collect only the dimensions needed, but cover all six for a full perspective Skill:

1. writings and long-form ideas;
2. long conversations and spontaneous reasoning;
3. short-form expression and recurring language;
4. external views and criticism;
5. actual decisions and behavior;
6. timeline and recent changes.

## Minimum bars

- Every core model: at least two independent supporting contexts, preferably primary.
- Every direct quote: verified against a locator.
- Every current claim: date-stamped and browsed.
- Every person Skill: at least one serious criticism or failure mode.
- Every learning card: source locator or explicit `inference` label.

If the corpus cannot meet the bar, reduce scope rather than invent certainty.

## Autonomous acquisition

When the user supplies only a name:

1. locate the official site and authored works;
2. locate full interviews or podcasts;
3. locate a representative short-form archive;
4. locate external criticism and decisions;
5. build the manifest before synthesis;
6. report missing dimensions.

Use summaries and links for distributable artifacts. Keep private full text local.
